Both Sides

One tool for the whole transaction.

Most paperwork tools assume you're the listing agent. CloseKit doesn't — it runs the side of the deal you're actually on.

Listing side

Take the listing to market

  • Listing agreement and seller property disclosure, filled and sent for signature.
  • Flood, lead-based paint, HOA, and HOPA disclosures — whichever the property calls for.
  • Co-sellers sign too. A document isn't done until every seller on it has signed.
  • When the offer arrives from the buyer's agent, the contract joins the same file.
Buyer side

Write the offer, not just file it

  • FAR/BAR built from your actual terms — price, financing, deposit, deadlines.
  • Countered? Update the terms and the contract regenerates. Signed documents are never altered.
  • Contract and applicable addenda go out as one signing package, in one session.
  • Co-buyers are full signers, with the same audit trail as everyone else.

Representing both parties? Pick dual agency at setup and CloseKit runs both tracks on a single transaction — one file, one timeline, every signature in one place.
